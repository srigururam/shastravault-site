Adhyāya I (प्रथमोऽध्यायः) — no title in the heading; colophon title "Kṛṣṇapurapraveśa"; 50 verses

I.1in the video

एकस्मिन् कमलाकटाक्षविवरे सानन्दगाधायिते
क्षीराब्धेरपरत्र दीर्घधवलैः कल्लोललोलोत्करैः ।
व्यग्रस्यापि समर्थितप्रमथनव्यापारलब्धोदया-
श्चत्वारो वितरन्तु वाञ्छितफलं वन्द्या हरेर्बाहवः ॥ १ ॥

May the four adorable arms of Hari bestow the fruit you desire. They are the arms of one who was busy. In one place they joyfully waded into ("found a ford in") the opening of Kamalā's (Lakṣmī's) sidelong glance. In another place they were among the long white masses of tossing waves of the Milk Ocean. They won their glory by carrying out the work of churning it.

Variants for सानन्दगाधायिते, P reads माधुर्यते (sense unclear; "becomes sweet"). This does not change the overall meaning.

I.2

नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।
देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ॥ २ ॥

Having bowed to Nārāyaṇa, to Nara the best of men, to the goddess Sarasvatī and to Vyāsa, one should then recite the Jaya (this sacred narrative).

Nārada said (नारद उवाच):

I.3

धर्माधर्मविधिः प्रोक्तो ब्रह्मन् सम्यक् श्रुतो मया ।
विद्याश्च विविधाः स्वामिन् वर्णभेदास्त्वनेकधा ॥ ३ ॥

O Brahman, you have explained the rules of dharma and adharma, and I have heard them properly. [You have also explained], O master, the various sciences (vidyā) and the many kinds of distinction among the varṇas.

I.4

मल्लविद्या त्वया प्रोक्ता मम संक्षेपतः पुरा ।
विस्तरेण मम ब्रूहि तस्यां कौतूहलं हि मे ॥ ४ ॥

Earlier you told me about the science of wrestling (mallavidyā) only in brief. Tell it to me in detail, for I am curious about it.

Variants B reads the first line as मल्लविद्या त्वया तात प्रोक्ता संक्षेपतः पुरा ("…told by you, O father, in brief before"). This only adds the vocative "father".

Brahmā said (ब्रह्मोवाच):

I.5

शृणु पुत्र शुभां विद्यां कथ्यमानां मयानघ ।
यदभ्यासान्नरः सम्यक् शत्रून् जयति सत्वरः ॥ ५ ॥

Listen, son, O sinless one, to the auspicious science as I tell it. A man who practises it properly swiftly conquers his enemies.

I.6in the video

पुरा कंसे हते दैत्ये चाणूरे च निपातिते ।
संप्राप्ते मथुराराज्ये भोगान् भुक्त्वा बहूंस्तथा ॥ ६ ॥

Long ago, the demon (daitya) Kaṃsa was slain, Cāṇūra was struck down and the kingdom of Mathurā was won. After [Kṛṣṇa and Balabhadra] had enjoyed many pleasures as well, …

Variants B reads दैवे for दैत्ये ("by fate", a likely miscopy). B reads तदा ("then") in the second line. P reads यथोक्तं गदते at one point (marker unclear). None of these changes the sense materially.

I.7in the video

कृष्णश्च बलभद्रश्च परमात्मदयान्वितौ ।
स्वीकर्तुं द्वारकां रम्यां चलितौ हर्षसंयुतौ ॥ ७ ॥

… Kṛṣṇa and Balabhadra, full of supreme compassion, joyfully set out to take possession of lovely Dvārakā.

I.8in the video

सुखप्रयाणकैर्देशान् पश्यन्तौ विविधान् बहून् ।
प्राप्तौ मयूरवं बालं नाम्ना नगरमुत्तमम् ॥ ८ ॥

Travelling in easy stages and seeing many different regions, the two reached an excellent city named Mayūravambāla (printed as two words, मयूरवं बालं).

I.9

त्रेतायां रामचन्द्रेण सीतामुत्सृज्य वाल्मिकी (जानकीम्) ।
पश्चाद् गुरुं समानीय प्रायश्चित्तं समाचरत् ॥ ९ ॥

In the Tretā age Rāmacandra abandoned Sītā. The printed text has "Vālmīki", which the editors correct to "Jānakī", so the line means "having abandoned Sītā, Janaka's daughter". Afterwards he brought his guru and undertook an expiation (prāyaścitta).

Variants footnote: B does not give ślokas 9–17, so I.9–17 rest on P alone.

I.10

अहो ब्रह्मन् त्व(म)या प्राप्तो रावणो ब्रह्मराक्षसः ।
तस्य पापस्य शुद्ध्यर्थमुपायं मे द्विजोत्तम ॥ १० ॥

[Rāma said:] "Alas, O Brahman, Rāvaṇa, a brahmarākṣasa, was overtaken by me." The text reads "by you", corrected by the editors to "by me". प्राप्तो literally means "reached" or "overtaken"; here it means he was killed. "[Tell] me, O best of the twice-born, the means of purifying that sin."

I.11

अश्वमेधस्य यज्ञस्य फलेन शुभमिच्छसि ।
हे राम सीतया सार्धं यज्ञश्च क्रियते त्वया ॥ ११ ॥

[The guru, no speaker label:] "You seek purity through the fruit of the Aśvamedha sacrifice. O Rāma, the sacrifice is to be performed by you together with Sītā."

I.12

सुवर्णमूर्तिं कृत्वा हि यज्ञं(ज्ञः) प्रारभ्यते त्वया ।
जाते यज्ञे च संपूर्णे ब्राह्मणांश्च प्रदापयेत् ॥ १२ ॥

"Make a golden image [of her], and the sacrifice is begun by you. When the sacrifice is complete, one should have gifts given to the Brāhmaṇas."

Note the text is still the guru's instruction (present/optative). It is not narrated as something already done.

I.13

ज्येष्ठांश्च कुण्डले दत्वा चातुर्वेदे च मुद्रिकाम् ।
द्विवेदिने स्त्री दातव्या चतुर्थे निष्कयस्तथा ॥ १३ ॥

"Give a pair of earrings (kuṇḍala) to the Jyeṣṭhas and a signet ring (mudrikā) to the Cāturveda (knower of four Vedas). A woman is to be given to the Dvivedin (knower of two Vedas). Likewise, to the fourth [class] give a niṣka (gold coin)."

Note निष्कय is printed as such; read निष्क.

I.14

आसनान्यङ्गवस्त्राणि तंदुलाः (तण्डुलाः) षट्कमेव च ।
एवं च रामचन्द्रेण मोहेरे स्थापिता पुरा ॥ १४ ॥

"[Also give] seats, body-cloths (aṅgavastra), rice grains (taṇḍula) and a set of six (ṣaṭka)." In this way [the settlement] was established long ago by Rāmacandra at Mohera (Moḍherā). The feminine स्थापिता suggests a feminine noun such as "settlement", "endowment" or "city".

I.15

सर्वेषां चैव विप्राणां वृत्तिं दत्वा विसर्जयेत् ।
कति वर्षाणि जातानि अन्योन्यं युद्धमेव च ॥ १५ ॥

"Give a livelihood (vṛtti) to all the Brāhmaṇas and dismiss them." Some years passed, and there was fighting among them.

I.16

ततः कुण्डलमुत्पाते यत्रस्थे तत्र जायते ।
किञ्चित्कालं तु निर्वृत्य पश्चाद्ग्राममयाचत ॥ १६ ॥

Then wherever [the holders of] the earrings (kuṇḍala) were, a disturbance arose there. When some time had passed, they afterwards asked for a village (grāma). (obscure line; tentative)

Note कुण्डल here means the earrings given in I.13, not the fire-pit (kuṇḍa). The first pāda is syntactically defective.

I.17

वयं सर्वेऽपि दुष्प्राप्य अग्रे किञ्च करिष्यसे ।
वरं कृष्णावतारे च द्वापरान्ते प्रदीयते ॥ १७ ॥

[The Brāhmaṇas:] "All of us have found [this] hard to obtain. What more will you do in future?" [Rāma:] "A boon is granted, [to be fulfilled] in the Kṛṣṇa incarnation at the end of the Dvāpara." (tentative)

Note the speakers are not marked, and दुष्प्राप्य is ungrammatical. Read it either as "having obtained with difficulty" or as "[it is] hard to obtain". I am unsure who says the first line.

I.18

प्रदत्तं ब्राह्मणान् पूर्वं रामेण स्थितिहेतवे ।
जपयज्ञाग्निहोत्रादिक्रियासंवादचेतसाम् ॥ १८ ॥

[This] was formerly granted by Rāma to the Brāhmaṇas for their maintenance (sthiti). Their minds were in accord with rites such as japa, sacrifice and agnihotra.

Variants from here on both P and B agree (editors' footnote), so B's omission covers 9–17 only.

Nārada said:

I.19

किं तन् मयूरवं बालं नाम्ना नगरमुत्तमम् ।
कस्मिन् देशेऽभवद्विद्वन् किं पुण्यं तद् वद प्रभो ॥ १९ ॥

What is that excellent city named Mayūravambāla? In what country was it, O learned one, and what is its merit? Tell me, Lord.

Variants B reads भवत् … for अभवद्विद्वन् (loses "O learned one"). P reads विपुण्यं (garbled).

Brahmā said:

I.20in the video

धर्मारण्यस्य मध्येऽभूत् शृणु नारद सादरम् ।
लोहासुरस्य पूर्वे च मोहेरस्य समीपतः ॥ २० ॥

Listen respectfully, Nārada. It lay in the middle of Dharmāraṇya, east of Lohāsura and near Mohera.

Variants P reads लेहासुर; P reads मोहेरस्य (मोरित). These are name spellings only.

Editors' note (front matter p. 8, fn 1; not part of the text) the editors identify Devālaya / Mayūravambāla with Delmāl in North Gujarat, about 10 miles west of Modherā. Their reasons are the step-wells, wells and ponds there, a small tank with an earthen dam on its east side, a broken Chaulukya-age temple on its west bank locally called Pālechī Mātā or Pālaṃmātā (compare Pālinī, I.32–34), many old temples, and the Jyeṣṭhīs who still lived there in 1964. They add that connecting the other age-names (Īśāna, Śrīmālaka, Mayūravambāla) with Delmāl "is difficult to explain".

I.21in the video

मयेदं स्थापितं पूर्वं विद्यास्थाने महाद्भुतम् ।
युतं द्विजगणैः शुद्धं सर्वरत्नसमन्वितम् ॥ २१ ॥

I established this most wonderful [city] long ago as a seat of learning (vidyāsthāna). It was filled with throngs of the twice-born, pure, and rich in every kind of jewel.

I.22in the video

स्वच्छोदकैः सुविस्तीर्णैः सरोभिरुपशोभितम् ।
आरामै रमणीयं च नानावृक्षसमाकुलैः ॥ २२ ॥

It was adorned with very broad lakes of clear water and made lovely by gardens crowded with many kinds of trees.

Variants B reads नानार्थ° ("of various kinds"). There is no change of sense.

I.23in the video

अनुलिप्तै(सं) सुधाभिश्च संगतं सुरमन्दिरैः ।
संकीर्णं पुण्यलभ्यं च रम्यं स्वर्गमिवापरम् ॥ २३ ॥

It was filled with temples of the gods plastered with lime-wash (sudhā). It was thronged, reachable only through merit, and delightful, like a second heaven.

Variants B reads अनुलिप्तैः ("plastered", agreeing with the temples).

I.24in the video

वातेरितपताकाभिः सेवितं च दिवानिशम् ।
युक्तमक्षय्यमानोर्थैर्ब्राह्मणैरुपशोभितम् ॥ २४ ॥

Day and night it was attended by banners stirred by the wind. It was graced by Brāhmaṇas whose wealth (artha) never fails (reading अक्षय्यमान-अर्थैः; the compound is slightly irregular).

I.25in the video

यज्ञाग्निधूमपटलैर्यत्रान्तरितमण्डलम् ।
न पीडयति तीक्ष्णांशुर्ग्रीष्मेऽपि च तपस्विनः ॥ २५ ॥

There the sky-disc is veiled by clouds of smoke from sacrificial fires, so that even in summer the fierce-rayed sun does not trouble the ascetics.

Variants B reads यमान्तरित°; B reads तपस्विनः variant (marker 16, garbled). There is no change of sense.

I.26

सुन्दरीगणसंपूर्णं वेदध्वनिसमाकुलम् ।
पवित्रं तीर्थभूतं च धर्मारण्यप्रदेशजम् ॥ २६ ॥

It was full of crowds of beautiful women and resounded with Vedic chanting. It was holy and itself a tīrtha, arising in the region of Dharmāraṇya.

Variants B reads तीर्थभूमिश्च ("and a land of tīrthas").

I.27in the video

कृते तु नाम्ना चेशानं त्रेतायां च श्रीमालकम् ।
द्वापरे मयूरवं बालं कलौ देवालयं स्मृतम् ॥ २७ ॥

In the Kṛta age it was called Īśāna, in the Tretā Śrīmālaka, in the Dvāpara Mayūravambāla, and in the Kali it is remembered as Devālaya.

Variants P gives different names for the first ages. Footnote 18 is partly illegible; it appears to read कृते सुनाम । रत्नाख्यं त्रेतायां कनकाख्यं [?] P, i.e., names like "Ratna" and "Kanaka". I am unsure of the exact P reading.

I.28

अधिश्रितं सुरैः सर्वैः पालितं पुण्यसंपदो ।
विद्याधरैः सिद्धगणैरप्सरोभिः समन्वितम् ॥ २८ ॥

All the gods dwell in it. It is protected, an abundance of merit (पुण्यसंपदो is syntactically loose), and filled with Vidyādharas, troops of Siddhas and Apsarases.

Variants P adds an extra śloka after this verse (footnote 20, marker at समन्वितम्): दक्षिणे ह्यभानुश्च[?] प्रतीच्यां ब्रह्मणः[?] स्थितः । उत्तरे च महावीरो विदिक्षु मातरस्तथा ॥. It means: "To the south [is] Bhānu (the Sun); to the west Brahmā is stationed; to the north Mahāvīra; and likewise the Mothers in the intermediate directions."

I.29

वापीकूपतडागैश्च पुण्यैर्जलयुतैर्युतम् ।
अनेकगुणपूर्णं च पुण्यं जननिषेवितम् ॥ २९ ॥

It was furnished with step-wells (vāpī), wells (kūpa) and tanks (taḍāga), holy and full of water. It was full of many virtues, sacred, and frequented by people.

I.30in the video

तत्पूर्वेऽस्ति वृद्धसरः पूर्णं पुण्यजलैः सदा ।
मयैव निर्मितं पूर्वं लोकपापापनुत्तये ॥ ३० ॥

To its east is the Vṛddhasaras ("Old/Ancient Lake"), always full of holy water. I myself made it long ago to wash away the sins of the world.

I.31

संचितं सारसैर्हंसैर्नानापक्षिगणैस्तथा ।
देवासुरमनुष्यैश्च तापसैर्योगसाधकैः ॥ ३१ ॥

It is thronged with cranes (sārasa), swans and many flocks of birds, and with gods, asuras, men, ascetics and practitioners of yoga, …

Variants footnote 21 (marker at संचितं) says "Not found in B". The marker's scope is probably this verse.

I.32

याज्ञिकैर्द्विजवर्यैश्च यक्षगन्धर्वसिद्धकैः ।
तत्प्रतीच्यां महादेवी पालिनीति च नामतः ॥ ३२ ॥

… with performers of sacrifice, the best of the twice-born, Yakṣas, Gandharvas and Siddhas. To its west is the Great Goddess named Pālinī, …

I.33

स्थापिता लोकरक्षार्थं तत्पूर्वे मातरः स्मृताः ।
तदन्तरालतीर्थानि सन्ति पुण्यान्यनेकशः ॥ ३३ ॥

… installed for the protection of the world. To its east the Mothers (mātaraḥ) are remembered. In the space between them there are many holy tīrthas.

Variants P reads पुण्यसहस्रशः ("by the thousand") for पुण्यान्यनेकशः.

I.34in the video

अस्मिन् सरोजले स्नात्वा देही पश्यति पालिनीम् ।
सर्वपापविनिर्मुक्तो लभते भोगमव्ययम् ॥ ३४ ॥

An embodied being who bathes in the water of this lake and sees Pālinī is freed from all sins and obtains imperishable enjoyment.

Variants P reads सरसि जले. The sense is the same.

I.35in the video

दृढाङ्गो रोगमुक्तश्च सर्वविद्याविचक्षणः ।
धनधान्यसमायुक्तो रिपुजेता महाबली ॥ ३५ ॥

[He becomes] firm of limb (dṛḍhāṅga), free from disease, expert in all sciences, endowed with wealth and grain, a conqueror of enemies, and very strong.

I.36

त्रेतायां रामचन्द्रेण कृतं स्नानं फलाप्तये ।
कृता यज्ञाश्च दानानि लोकसंग्रहणाय वै ॥ ३६ ॥

In the Tretā age Rāmacandra bathed here to gain its fruit. He also performed sacrifices and gave gifts for the welfare of the world (lokasaṃgraha).

I.37

किं वर्ण्यतेऽस्य श्रेष्ठस्य तडागस्य फलश्रुतिम्(तिः) ।
संक्षेपात् कथितं तात वृद्धस्य सरसः फलम् ॥ ३७ ॥

How can the phalaśruti of this excellent tank be described [in full]? I have told you the fruit of the Vṛddha lake in brief, dear one.

I.38in the video

एतन्नगरपार्श्वे तु परितश्च प्रदक्षिणे ।
बहुदेवालयाः सन्ति तेन देवालयं स्मृतम् ॥ ३८ ॥

Beside this city, all around it and along the circuit (pradakṣiṇa), there are many temples (devālaya). For this reason it is remembered as Devālaya.

Variants B reads देवालकं (name form). P reads बृहदेवाश्रया (marker 24; "having great temples").

I.39

ख्यातं चतुर्षु लोकेषु विद्यास्थानं परं सदा ।

तस्माद् गृह्णात्वेनां त्वं मल्लविद्यां द्विजोत्तम ।
यया च भ्रामिताः सर्वे शत्रवो निधनं गताः ॥ ४९ ॥

"Therefore, O best of the twice-born, take this mallavidyā, through which all the enemies were whirled about (bhrāmita) and went to their death."

Note (revised 2026-11-03) the print reads गृह्णात्वेनां (gṛhṇātu enām, a 3rd-person imperative used with त्वं), not गृहाणेमां as earlier transcribed. The sense, "take it", is unchanged.

II.50

इति ते कथिता ब्रह्मन् मल्लविद्या मयानघ ।
किं पृच्छसि पुनः स्वामिन् कथयामि तव प्रियाम् ॥ ५० ॥

"Thus, O Brāhmaṇa, O sinless one, I have told you about mallavidyā. What more do you ask, master? I shall tell you what is dear to you."

यः शृणोति नरो भक्त्या मल्लकंसादिघातनम् ।
क्रीडितं मम विप्रेन्द्र स याति परमं पदम् ॥ ५१ ॥

"O chief of Brāhmaṇas, the man who listens with devotion to my sport (krīḍita), the slaying of the wrestlers, Kaṃsa and the rest, reaches the supreme state."
