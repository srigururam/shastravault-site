How we work

A story you can check.

Our videos are made to be gripping. Our pages are made to be checked. Neither invents anything.

१

Every claim has a verse

Each line we narrate points to a verse number, shown on screen and listed here.

२

Gaps stay gaps

Where pages are missing, we show the hole. We never fill it with guesses.

३

Our words are marked

When we add commentary, we say it's ours. Doubtful readings are flagged as doubtful.

४

Honest about tools

Translations are AI-assisted and checked against the printed original. Voices and paintings are AI-made.
